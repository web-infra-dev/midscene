## A new E2E testing framework designed for natural language {#midscene-test}

Midscene Test (`@midscene/test`) is designed from the ground up for natural-language E2E testing. Write actions and assertions in YAML, extend test steps with TypeScript Nodes, and manage setup, cleanup, retries, and execution reports within one framework. [Get started with Midscene Test](./midscene-test/use)

### AI and business operations in one YAML file {#ai-and-nodes}

Each YAML step calls a Node: an AI operation, a platform operation, or a custom business operation. Compose these Nodes into test cases that people and AI agents can read, write, and review.

### Customize your test project {#nodes-and-spec}

Extend Midscene Test with TypeScript Nodes for your business operations. Register them to make them callable from YAML, then generate a Markdown Node Spec describing all Nodes registered for the current execution project, including AI, platform, and custom business operations and their inputs. People and AI agents can use the Spec to reference these Nodes in test cases. [Develop custom Nodes](./midscene-test/extend)
